## Context

ADR 0012 recorded a reduced S5/S11 run on synthetic block scores because the
predefined `cmp_null` and `cmp_effect` worlds were not frozen in-repo. The
worlds now use the standard decks, brackets and paired opponent pool, S11 adds
the SC4 interaction world and an independently enumerated target, and the S5
state variants have prescribed-replication counts.

## Worlds

`podrank/sim/worlds/cmp_null.json` and `cmp_effect.json` are frozen with seed
707, agents `agent_00` (A) and `agent_01` (B), standard seats, 5% draws and 1%
caps.

- The pod support is 64 pods frozen by seed 707 from all four standard brackets
  and their 12-deck pools.
- Duplicate uses B in all three opponent seats.
- Paired uses every one of the 27 ordered triples from `agent_00`–`agent_02`.
- Each cell stores `theta_A` for duplicate and paired; recalculation over the
  support reproduces the target lift.
- S11 cell `k` freezes the SC4 interactions from
  `SeedSequence(707, spawn_key=(4, k))`: independent agent×deck and ordered
  agent×opponent effects `N(0, 0.3²)`, plus the 0.2 kingmaker penalty on the
  highest additive utility.

## S5 results

72 cells: 3 boundary conditions × 2 designs × 4 `ρ_div` × 3 failure
configurations, each with **10,000 replications × 4,000 blocks**, `alpha = 0.05`,
`min_blocks_for_failure_rule = 50`, `max_failed_fraction = 0.02`.

- False-claim limit: `alpha + 2·MC SE = 0.0544`.
- **No cell exceeded the limit in either checked direction.**
- `superiority_002` checks the upper direction only; the other two cells check
  both directions.

## Consequences

- S5 and S11 now use the frozen standard-deck worlds, the paired opponent pool,
  the SC4 interaction world and the prescribed replication counts.
- No S11 cell is flagged and no S5 cell exceeds the boundary limit. Nothing was
  tuned to reach this; the interaction targets are reported as enumerated.
- Real-log validation remains an R2 release item.

## Alternatives considered

- Keep the fixed-pod analytic model. Rejected: it did not use the standard
  deck/bracket support or opponent pool.
- Compare S11 means with the nominal lift. Rejected: the SC4 world’s target is
  the interaction-world expectation.
- Leave the state variants as unit tests only. Rejected: the study now records
  prescribed-replication counts.
